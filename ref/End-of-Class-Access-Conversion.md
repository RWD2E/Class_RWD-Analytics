# Conversion to Long-term HABITAT Access

Your access to our NextGen data lake will **expire at the end of the semester**. If you would like to retain your access to our **de-identified** data lake, please following the steps below to convert to a study-specific account with dedicated, long-term access to our data lake (Snowflake and AWS Research Studio).

## Step 1. Obtain IRB Approval or Determination
**Request portal**: 
> https://ecompliance.missouri.edu/

The type of IRB submission required depends on the data access level needed for your project.

### Option A. Non-Human Subjects Research Determination
If your project can be conducted entirely using de-identified data and investigators will not have access to direct identifiers, dates of service, dates of birth, addresses, medical record numbers, or other HIPAA identifiers, you may be eligible for a Non-Human Subjects Research (NHSR) determination. Here are sample language that you can use to describe the de-identified database access on HABITAT: 

```
The study will utilize data from the NextGen Precision Health Research Data Lake (IRB#2072551). The dataset has been fully de-identified in accordance with HIPAA standards, and investigators will not have access to any HIPAA identifiers or any information that could reasonably be used to identify individuals. Data are provided solely for secondary research purposes and cannot be linked back to individual patients by study personnel.
```

### Option B. IRB Exemption (Category 4)
If your project requires access to a Limited Dataset (LDS) or other identifiable clinical data elements not available within the de-identified environment, you must submit an IRB application and may be eligible for Exempt Category 4 review. Here are sample language that you can use to describe the LDS data access on HABITAT: 

```
This study involves secondary research using existing clinical and administrative data maintained within the NextGen Precision Health Research Data Lake (IRB#2072551). Investigators will access only the minimum necessary information required to conduct the study, including dates and some geographical information below state. Data access will occur within approved secure research computing environment (HABITAT), and no attempt will be made to identify or contact individual patients. The study qualifies for Exempt Category 4 because it involves secondary research use of identifiable private information in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.
```


## Step 2. CBMI Intake Process
**Request portal:** 
> https://showmeportal.missouri.edu/redcap/surveys/?s=MCY9TRFEFPWDJ3EF 

Complete all applicable forms required to regain access to HABITAT resources. Depending on your project needs, you may request access to one or more of the following:

- i2b2 Cohort Discovery Tool
- MU De-identified or LDS Data Lake + Remote Desktop Environment (HABITAT)

Please note:

- Each resource requires a separate access request and approval workflow.
- Additional training, data use agreements, or supervisor approvals may be required depending on the requested access level.
- Access requests should reference the IRB protocol or NHSR determination obtained in Step 1.


## Step 3. Activate Accounts 

Once both the IRB/NHSR process and CBMI intake process have been completed and approved, you will receive one or more account activation emails.Follow the instructions in those emails to activate your accounts and establish access to the approved HABITAT resources. After activation:

- You will retain access as long as your project remains active and compliant with applicable governance requirements.
- Access is restricted to the specific study and data resources approved through your request.
- The HABITAT de-identified data lake is refreshed on a regular basis (typically monthly) to incorporate newly available clinical data.
- Continued access is subject to annual review, compliance monitoring, and project status updates as required by institutional policies.
